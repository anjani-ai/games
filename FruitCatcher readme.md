# Fruit Catcher 🍎🍌💣

**Fruit Catcher** is a fun and fast-paced game where you control a basket to catch falling fruits while avoiding bombs. Test your reflexes and see how high you can score before running out of lives!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Use the **Left** and **Right** arrow keys to move the basket.
2. **Catch the Fruits**: Gain points by catching fruits in your basket.
3. **Avoid the Bombs**: The game ends if you catch a bomb.
4. **Lives**: You lose a life if you miss a fruit. The game ends when your lives reach 0.

## 🚀 Features

- **Dynamic Difficulty**: Fruits and bombs fall faster as your score increases.
- **Lives System**: Lose a life for every fruit you miss.
- **Customizable Graphics**: Replace the basket, fruits, and bombs with your own images for a personalized experience.
