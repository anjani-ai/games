# Space Dodger 🚀

**Space Dodger** is a fun and addictive game where you control a spaceship and dodge falling asteroids. Test your reflexes and see how high you can score as the game gets faster and more challenging over time!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Use the **Left** and **Right** arrow keys to move your spaceship.
2. Dodge the falling asteroids to increase your score.
3. The game ends when an asteroid hits your spaceship.
4. Try to beat your high score!

## 🚀 Features

- **Dynamic Difficulty**: The game gets progressively harder as your score increases.
- **Simple Controls**: Easy to play with just two keys.
- **Customizable**: Replace the spaceship and asteroid shapes with images for a personalized touch.
